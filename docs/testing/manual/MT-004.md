# MT-004: Mac host power pull, network pull and disk full

Proves that a Mac host recovers unattended and that builds survive it. Real power loss, a real cable pull and a real full disk cannot be simulated faithfully, so a person does them.

## Preconditions

- An enrolled, Online host with a macOS pool and a golden image, and physical access to its power and Ethernet cables.
- A reference workload that keeps the macOS VMs busy for at least 20 minutes (for example a build with many macOS actions), and an admin session.
- Know the host's configured `unreachable_limit` for VMs (default 10 minutes).
- Evidence rules: [`README.md`](README.md#evidence).

## Steps

1. [agent] Start the workload and confirm VMs are busy: `cucinactl workers list --pool <macos pool>`.
2. [human] **Power pull.** Disconnect the power cable (no clean shutdown), count ten seconds, reconnect it. Do not touch anything else.
3. [agent] Poll `cucinactl hosts list` every 30 seconds. Record when the host turns Offline, when it is Online again, and when its VM workers have re-registered.
4. [agent] The build completes successfully: interrupted operations were retried (`cucinactl ops list` showed them requeued) and no more than two VMs ever ran on the host.
5. [agent] After recovery the VM disks are intact (`tart list` on the host) and a new action runs on the same VMs.
6. [human] **Network pull.** With actions running, unplug Ethernet for five minutes, then reconnect.
7. [agent] The controller marks the host Offline and its in-flight actions are retried elsewhere or queued. After reconnection `cucinactl hosts list` shows Online, hostd's log shows a reconnect with a full resync, and the build completes.
8. [human] Repeat step 6 with an outage longer than the VMs' `unreachable_limit` (for example 12 minutes). The VMs power themselves off on their own, and after reconnection new work starts them again.
9. [human] **Disk full.** Fill the host's data volume until less than 5 GiB is free, using the host itself, for example `mkfile 200g /Users/Shared/cucina-fill.bin` sized to the free space.
10. [agent] `cucinactl hosts list` shows the low free space. Request work for the macOS pool: hostd must refuse to pull images or start VMs with a clear error (visible in `cucinactl hosts diag <serial>`), prune images within its budget, and must not crash-loop. Builds route elsewhere or wait; they do not fail with obscure errors.
11. [human] Delete the fill file. Hostd recovers by itself and the next VM starts without intervention.

## Expected results

- Power pull and network pull: the host returns and re-registers unattended; no build fails; the two-VM cap holds.
- A long outage makes VMs shut themselves down rather than run orphaned.
- A full disk produces a clear, observable refusal, never a crash loop, and recovery needs no manual step.

## Required evidence

- The timeline from step 3 (Offline, Online, registered) and from step 7, with timestamps.
- `cucinactl hosts list` and `hosts diag` output for step 10.
- The build's final result and the list of requeued operations.
- Any defect as an issue; a defect that can be automated becomes a regression test.
